# Data Warehouse and Aanlytics Project
# Advanced SQL Data Warehouse & Business Analytics

## Definition

An advanced SQL project focused on transforming retail operational data into a structured analytical database using **Data Warehouse concepts, relational modeling, and advanced SQL techniques**.

The project aims to build a **Star Schema** consisting of fact and dimension tables, then use advanced SQL queries, views, and stored procedures to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and business trends.

---

## Business Scenario

A retail organization wants to transform its operational data into an analytical relational database for **executive reporting and KPI monitoring**.

The database should support business analysis and help decision-makers understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and sales trends.

---

## Required Tasks

### 1. Database Design

* Analyze the provided retail dataset.
* Identify the main business entities and relationships.
* Normalize the dataset into fact and dimension tables.
* Build a **Star Schema**.
* Define appropriate data types for all columns.

### 2. Database Relationships

* Create **Primary Keys (PK)**.
* Create **Foreign Keys (FK)**.
* Establish relationships between fact and dimension tables.
* Maintain referential integrity.

### 3. Advanced SQL Queries

Write analytical SQL queries using:

* `JOIN`
* Subqueries
* `CTE`
* `CASE`
* Aggregate Functions
* `GROUP BY`
* `HAVING`
* Window Functions

The queries should answer meaningful business questions.

### 4. KPI Analysis

Calculate and analyze business KPIs such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Average Order Value
* Number of Orders
* Number of Customers
* Sales Growth
* Customer Contribution
* Top Performing Products
* Regional Performance

### 5. SQL Views

* Create at least **one SQL View**.
* The view should provide reusable analytical data for reporting or KPI analysis.

### 6. Stored Procedures

* Create at least **one Stored Procedure**.
* The procedure should perform a reusable analytical or KPI calculation.
* Use parameters where appropriate.

### 7. Business Analytics

Create analytical reports covering:

#### Profitability Analysis

* Revenue and Profit
* Profit Margin
* Most and least profitable products
* Regional profitability

#### Customer Behavior

* Customer spending
* Purchase frequency
* Top customers
* Customer segmentation

#### Sales Trends

* Monthly and yearly sales
* Sales growth
* Product performance over time
* Regional sales trends

### 8. Query Optimization

Optimize SQL queries by:

* Avoiding unnecessary columns.
* Using appropriate filtering.
* Creating suitable indexes.
* Reviewing execution plans where applicable.
* Reducing unnecessary calculations.
* Comparing query performance before and after optimization.

---

## Technical Requirements

* Minimum **5 relational tables**.
* At least **15 SQL queries**.
* Minimum **3 JOIN operations**.
* At least **2 CTEs**.
* At least **1 stored procedure**.
* At least **1 SQL view**.
* At least **1 Star Schema**.
* At least **1 Fact Table**.
* At least **3 Dimension Tables**.
* Use **Primary Keys and Foreign Keys**.
* Include **Subqueries and CASE statements**.
* Include analytical KPIs and business insights.
